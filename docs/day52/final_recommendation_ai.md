# Day 52 – Final Recommendation AI

## Objective
Produce Selected, Hold / Review or Rejected recommendations from the unified score and risk factors.

## Logic
- Missing scores or insufficient coverage result in Hold / Review.
- High or unverified risk factors require human investigation.
- Otherwise configurable thresholds determine the recommendation band.
- Confidence is a coverage-based heuristic, not a statistical probability.

## Safety
This is decision support only. Every recommendation requires human review; no autonomous hiring or rejection occurs.
