# Day 58 – Advanced Feature Proposal

## Objective
Propose future capabilities and an incremental architecture roadmap.

## Opportunity areas from the task brief
- AI video analysis, emotion detection and real-time feedback.
- AI coaching, candidate improvement suggestions and interview analytics dashboard.

## Governance and feasibility
Video/emotion features are high-risk and should not infer employability, personality or honesty. Any optional video processing needs explicit informed consent, a non-video alternative, purpose limitation, retention controls and bias validation. Prefer explainable transcript-based coaching and recruiter analytics first.

## Roadmap
1. Foundation: event contracts, consent, data minimization and audit.
2. Near term: candidate coaching from rubric-linked feedback and recruiter dashboards.
3. Later: real-time feedback after latency and accessibility testing.
4. Research gate: video/emotion analysis only after legal, privacy, fairness and independent validation review.
