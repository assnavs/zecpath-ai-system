# Experience Relevance Scoring

## Overview

The Experience Relevance Scorer evaluates how closely a candidate's work experience matches the target job role.

## Objectives

- Compare job roles
- Calculate relevance score
- Detect similar job titles
- Evaluate total experience
- Identify employment gaps
- Detect overlapping employment

## Scoring Logic

| Criteria | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Exact Role Match | 50 |
| Similar Role Match | 30 |
| 5+ Years Experience | 30 |
| 3–5 Years Experience | 20 |
| 1–3 Years Experience | 10 |

Maximum Score = 100

## Features

- Role similarity matching
- Experience-based scoring
- Employment gap detection
- Overlapping job detection
- Structured relevance output

## Benefits

- ATS-friendly candidate ranking
- Better role matching
- Consistent evaluation
