# Day 53 – Hiring Intelligence Report Generator

## Objective
Create a recruiter-friendly profile combining ATS, screening, HR, technical, machine-test, behavioral and integrity data.

## Output
Candidate metadata, supplied round summaries, strengths, weaknesses, risk indicators, unified score, recommendation, UTC timestamp and limitations.

## Data handling
Missing sections remain null. The generator does not infer results. Risk flags are review context, not findings.
